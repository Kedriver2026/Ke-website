Driver Onboarding

K&E DELIVERY

Independent Contractor Agreement

Review the agreement below, then sign electronically.

1. Independent Contractor Relationship

The Driver performs delivery services as an independent contractor and not as an employee of K&E DELIVERY.

2. Voluntary Route Acceptance

Routes may be offered through K&E Connect. The Driver may choose whether to accept an available route.

3. Vehicle, Operating Expenses, and Airport Parking

The Driver is responsible for providing and maintaining an appropriate vehicle and for operating expenses unless K&E DELIVERY expressly agrees otherwise in writing. K&E DELIVERY will reimburse the Driver $7.00 for airport parking for each completed route pickup. This reimbursement applies to airport parking only and does not include tolls, fuel, vehicle maintenance, or other operating or travel expenses unless specifically authorized by K&E DELIVERY.

4. Compensation

Compensation for delivery work will be established or displayed by K&E DELIVERY.

5. Baggage and Delivery Responsibilities

The Driver must safeguard passenger baggage, follow delivery instructions, maintain professional conduct, and promptly report incidents.

6. Confidentiality and Passenger Information

Passenger and delivery information may be used only to perform authorized delivery services.

7. K&E Connect and WMS

The Driver must protect account credentials and use K&E Connect, WMS, and related systems only for authorized business purposes.

8. Accepted Routes and Cancellations

Once a route is accepted, the Driver is expected to complete it according to K&E DELIVERY service requirements.

9. Accidents and Incidents

The Driver must promptly report accidents, injuries, baggage loss or damage, security incidents, and other significant events.

10. Taxes

The Driver is responsible for federal, state, and local tax obligations arising from independent contractor compensation.

11. Compliance and Safety

The Driver must comply with applicable laws, airport and airline requirements, traffic and safety rules.

12. Suspension or Termination

K&E DELIVERY may suspend or terminate access for material violations, safety or security concerns, fraud, or failure to safeguard baggage, subject to applicable law.
