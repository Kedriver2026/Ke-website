THE DRIVER SIDE OF K&E

Built around drivers. Connected to the operation.

K&E Connect is the home for our driver experience. The goal is simple: give drivers a clear, professional place to understand the work, accept routes, stay connected with dispatch, get the right help in the field and grow with K&E as we expand.

WHY K&E CONNECT

We want drivers to feel supported — not just assigned.

01

Clear From the Start

Know what the route is, where you're going and what is expected before you head out. Less confusion. Better communication.

02

Support in the Field

K&E Connect is designed to keep the important information close while you're working — airport instructions, route guidance, delivery help and direct support.

03

Grow With the Family

As K&E grows into new markets, we want strong drivers growing with us. K&E Connect is part of building that long-term driver relationship.

TECHNOLOGY WITH A HUMAN SIDE

The app helps. The people behind it matter more.

K&E Connect is not meant to replace communication with drivers. It is meant to make that communication better. The platform supports the workflow, while the K&E team remains behind the driver with real operational support. Technology keeps us connected; trust keeps the family strong.

WHERE WE'RE HEADING

Building the next generation of the K&E driver experience.

We are building K&E Connect step by step around the real work our drivers do. Route acceptance, airport arrival, delivery progress, maps, support, earnings visibility and driver assistance all belong in one connected experience.
